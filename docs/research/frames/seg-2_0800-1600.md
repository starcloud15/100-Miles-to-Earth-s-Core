**Miles accumulate about 0.08 mi per minute per displayed MPH.**
- 28 MPH gives about 2.2–2.4 mi/min; 20 MPH about 1.4–1.5; 10 MPH about 0.9.
- So the Miles counter runs about **4.8× faster than the MPH number implies**. The "miles" are game miles, and the counter is tuned for fast progress.
- 0.1 mi ticks every 2.5 s at 30 MPH, every 4 s at 20, and every 6–7 s at 9–11.

**Whole slice:** 6.9 mi at 8:00 to 14.7 mi at 15:36. That is 7.8 mi in 7.6 min, **1.03 mi/min including 2.6 min of stops**.
