### 3.2 What the numbers mean

**Miles are not real miles at the shown MPH.**

- **Rule of thumb: game miles per minute ≈ MPH ÷ 12.8.** One game mile takes about 2 min at 6 MPH, 28 s at 27 MPH and 13 s at 60 MPH.

**What changes speed:**
