## 5. The world

**Gator Swamp (16:00–22:50, 14.7–19.8 mi):**
- **Water:** opaque murky olive-green (#5f8f6a to #8aa57f) with a pale Voronoi caustic pattern. Foam trails behind the raft.
- **Fog:** heavy teal-green (#4f8f7f), visibility about 150–250 studs. Distant banks are flat silhouettes.
- **Banks:** low, grassy and muddy, with reeds and cattail clusters.
- **Midstream obstacles:** big mossy grey stone pyramids and blocks (2–4 raft lengths across).
- **Other props:** lily pads, tilted dead trunks, and yellow diamond gator signs on posts.
- **Dusk (18:15–19:00):** the whole fog shifts to salmon-pink, then deep red (#c06a5a), and the sun badge turns orange. A strong mood change that also says "hurry".
- **Night (19:50–21:05):** blue-black (#101820) with fog. Only the fire glow and lanterns are lit, plus white searchlight beams cutting in from outside the ring.
- **Dawn (21:07):** cold slate-blue light.

**Frostbite Pass (from 22:56, about 20 mi):**
- **Water:** bright pale cyan (#8fd6ea) and translucent, with white crackle lines (caustics). Floating ice slabs and white mist plumes.
- **Banks:** tall, steep, faceted blue-grey rock walls (#4a5f80), granite-speckled texture, snow ledges. The channel is about 3–5 raft widths.
- **Skyline:** snow-capped blocky pine silhouettes along the cliff tops.
- **Light:** bright clear sky, low fog. Very high contrast with the swamp.
- **Transition:** about 10 s of colour change at 19.8–20.1 mi.

**Why it looks rich:** strong per-zone colour grading (fog colour, water colour and light all change together), dense mid-distance silhouettes, rocks and signs in the channel, and dusk and night tinting the whole screen.
