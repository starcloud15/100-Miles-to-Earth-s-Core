## 2. Rhythm: the numbers that matter

### 2.1 Zone lengths, measured from the route bar

**The marker:**
- The white marker is **linear in miles**: about 5.585 px per mile at 2560×1440, with Mile 0 at x≈166.6 of the 560 px track and the yellow end tick at **Mile 100**.
- Every reading fits within ±0.5 mi. For example: 9 mi at 217 px, 20.4 mi at 280.5 px, 39.2 mi at 385.5 px.

## 3. Systems inventory

### 3.1 River and raft
- The river is a guided channel with a current. The banks are walls of rock or cliff, but the channel is wide enough to steer: avoid rocks and ice floes, pick a side, reach floating loot.
- Click to paddle. Every crew member's paddle adds speed. Paddling on one side turns the raft (judgement from the footage).
- The raft is an **open wooden platform with no walls**. Players stand anywhere, can be knocked off by impacts or monsters, swim, and get swept back.
- Raft health: "Repair your raft with wood!" at camps.
- Upgrades seen: corner posts with lanterns, barrels or floats, a slightly larger deck.
- Beaching: the raft stops on sandbars. Fix it with Push Off (19 R$) or by paddling and pushing.

### 3.2 Survival
- Hunger drains steadily, faster when paddling (judgement). Health drops from monsters and falls.
- Food ladder: Raw fish, then Cooked Fish ("way more than raw"), then Fish Stew (fills more and lasts longer).

### 3.3 Activities
| Activity | How it works |
|---|---|
| Punching trees | No axe at the start. Fists, "Click Punch". Logs pop out (+10) and go into the Bag. |
| Wood | Repairs the raft, fuels the fire (+0.1× each) and builds the raft at the start. |
| Fishing | Rod in hotbar slot 2. Cast, "…" wait, "! CLICK" bite, reel, or "Got away". Works from the raft or the dock. |
| Cooking | Cookfire panel: recipe list, selected recipe detail, ingredient count, **hold-to-cook** green bar. |
| Eating and dropping | Holding food shows "Click Eat · Q Drop". Items can be dropped for crewmates. |
| Looting | Chests and crates at landings and camps, with item toasts ("Wooden Chest: 3 Fish · Eat it to fill your hunger"). |
| Lore | Journal notes pop up as a "NEW!" paper card with a sketch and a one-line clue signed by a faction (Settlers, Steamboat Crew, Trappers, Scientists). They feed the Journal collection in the lobby. |

### 3.4 Threats
| Threat | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Signature stalker** | A towering, gaunt shadow figure with very long arms and claws, white pin-point eyes and lightning around it. It "fears fire", hunts at night and in storms, stalks the camp ring and sweeps light beams. It's teased in the intro and shown again at every night camp, but it **never kills on screen**. It's pressure. |
| **Zone river threat** | Gators. "Gators hunt swimmers." They grab you with the SPAM! QTE and knock players off the raft. |
| **Environmental** | Rapids, rocks, ice floes ("KNOCKED OFF!"), sandbars, and a large creature that smashes the raft (jungle). |

### 3.5 Meta
- Currency earned per run (flame drop).
- Leaderboards: fastest run, currency earned, total miles.
- Classes, a badge per zone, the Journal collection.
- Free Revive for joining the group and liking the game: a growth loop.
- Invite button, pets (Starter Pack).

## 5. What the reference does well, and where we can beat it

**Keep:**
- One honest number (miles) that always moves.
- A goal that's always 1–3 min away: the next camp, with a distance on screen.
- A deadline that sets the tempo ("before dark").
- A safe circle with a crew-shared speed-up (the fire multiplier turns waiting into a job).
- The monster as constant pressure, not constant death.
- Lore notes as a collection.
- The % funnel as a dare.
- Contextual, cheap Robux prompts sitting next to the problem they solve.

**Beat it on (all judgement, backed by the Run a Resort data):**
1. **Time to control.** 65 s against a 5–10 s target. Cut the party-pad wait for solo players and keep the cutscene to 8 s or put it in the world. The Run a Resort cutscene test showed 60% skip within 3 s.
2. **Bright start.** The reference keeps its first minute bright, then goes dark at 2:50. Ours must too: the surface zone starts at noon.
3. **Saves.** No checkpoint was visible, and 2 of 4 players left at about 13 min. Add **Waystations** that save the expedition so a 100-mile trip spans several sessions. That is the D1 hook.
4. **Crew drop-off.** When players leave, the raft still needs paddlers. Add NPC rowers or scale paddle power to crew size.
5. **Variety per stop.** Landings mostly repeat chests and fire. Give every stop one unique thing: a trader, a puzzle, a rescue, a fork.
6. **The Push Off price.** Charging Robux to get unstuck reads as a toll. Keep a free crew push (hold F together for 3 s) and sell instant push only as a convenience.
