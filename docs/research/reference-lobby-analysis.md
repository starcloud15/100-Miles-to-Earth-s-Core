## 2. Party and matchmaking (the player-count answer)

| Item | What the reference does |
|---|---|
| **Party size** | The creator picks **1, 2, 3, 4 or 5**. **Max 5**, and **4 is selected by default** (recording). |
| **Friends Only** | A tickbox. Unticked, anyone can walk onto the pad and join (recording). |
| **Pads** | 6 or more glass pads in a row on the riverbank. Empty: white outline, "PLAY / Step in to make a party". Taken: green glow, "N/M PLAYERS / Starting in Xs", then the leader's name. Yours: yellow outline (recording). |
| **Countdown** | Starts at about **20 s** as soon as the party is made. People joining **do not reset it**. When the party **fills**, it **drops to 5 s** (2:16 at 11 s → 2:18 at 5 s, full) (recording). |
| **Under-filled parties start anyway** | "1/4 PLAYERS, Starting in 7s" counts down to zero. A solo run is allowed (recording, inferred from the timer running). |
| **Launch** | "STARTING! Teleporting…", then everyone on the pad goes to a run server (recording). |
| **Big PLAY button** | Bottom centre, always on screen. Probably quick play to the nearest open party (inferred; not pressed in the clip). |
| **Parties seen** | 3/4, 1/4, 1/4 and 3/3 (Friends Only). **None bigger than 4** (recording). |
| **Lobby server** | The player list shows 14+ names and scrolls, with a "Miles" column (pretty_anne 87.2, ley 81.9…) (recording). |

**Why it works** (judgement):
- Nobody waits more than about 20 s, which protects first-play bounce. A full party waits only 5 s.
- Public pads let solo players get a crew from strangers. Friends Only lets friends lock theirs, which feeds the co-play signal: "playing with friends via join, invite or private servers; reserved servers count since Dec 2025" (Run a Resort KB, official).
- A default of 4 with a max of 5 keeps every crew member useful on a small raft.

## 9. Social and growth hooks
- **Invite** button in the HUD, and an **Invite Friends** button on empty Friends leaderboards (recording).
- **Free Revive for joining the group and liking the game.** It's a two-step checklist that ticks as you go; the like step only updates after you rejoin (recording).
  - **Caution (judgement):** rewarding a *like* may break Roblox's rules on incentivised votes. Check the current Community Standards before copying it. Rewarding a group join is common and widely accepted.
- **Favorite prompt** (the Roblox PromptSetFavorite dialog) appears in the lobby at 2:31, after returning from a run (recording; trigger timing inferred).
- **Fastest Run is about 51 min for 100 miles** (global top 10 is 51:06 to 55:45) (recording). In the gameplay recording a normal run averages about 0.84 mi/min, which is about 2 h, so **top crews run about twice as fast as a normal crew** (judgement from both recordings).

## 11. What we take for 100 Miles to Earth's Core (proposal, needs owner approval)

We take the reference lobby's **structure**, not its content. Our own currency, classes and items are in [GDD section 14](../GameDesignDocument.md), which replaces 11.3 and 11.4 below where they differ.

The lobby is the **surface camp at the mouth of the dig**. Players start on the grass in daylight, so the first thing they see is the hole they're about to go down (judgement).

### 11.1 Player count
- **Crew of 1–5. The party creator picks the size, with 4 selected by default.** This is the same as the reference, and no party bigger than 4 was seen in either recording (recording). It replaces the GDD's "1–6".
- **Solo is allowed.** A badge of our own for reaching the Core alone ("Lone Driller"), on the same idea as the reference's solo badge.
- **Run servers:** one reserved server per party. Friends who start together through a reserved server count for the co-play signal (Run a Resort KB, official since Dec 2025).
- **Lobby servers: about 24 players.** The reference showed 14+ in the list. That's enough strangers to fill public parties quickly (judgement).
- With the two-tread rig, an odd crew still works: the spare player helps the weaker side, and the HUD shows which side needs help (judgement).

### 11.2 Getting into a run fast
- **Launch bays** (our pads) work like the reference's:
  - Step in to create a party: pick a size from 1 to 5, and tick Friends Only if you want.
  - The **20 s countdown never resets**, drops to **5 s when the party is full**, and an under-filled party starts anyway.
- **The big PLAY button** puts you in the fullest open public party. If none exists, it starts a 1-player party with the 20 s timer.
- **New players spawn facing the bays,** with a bouncing arrow on PLAY and no panels open. That way a first-timer is on the way down well inside 60 s (judgement, from the bounce data in the Run a Resort KB).

### 11.3 Rewards paid at camp
- The Journal and Badges are claimed at camp with a big "+N" pop-up, as in the reference.
- **Currency:** one meta currency of our own, **Deepstones** (the reference's is Embers).
- **Badge rewards** follow the same curve as the reference: 25 / 25 / 75 / 200 for distance, and 200 for challenges.

### 11.4 Classes: the reference's price ladder, our own roles

| Class | Price | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| Prospector | 150 / 49 R$ | Starts with an Iron Pickaxe, +1 glowstone per vein |
| Forager | 750 / 149 R$ | Starts with an Iron Trowel, wider green zone on every Pull, food fills 15% more |
| Lamplighter | 1500 / 299 R$ | Starts with a Hand Lantern; the Beacon and lanterns burn glowstone 15% slower |

- Perks stay small and help the crew.
- Previews show the outfit on your own avatar.

### 11.5 Collection
- A Journal with **6 notes and 2 pages per zone**. Collections are the main reason players come back on days 2–7.
- A **13-badge set** in the same three groups:
  - **Distance:** Mile 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 (the Core).
  - **Challenges:** no knock-downs, solo to the Core, the full Journal.
  - **Crew:** invite a friend, revive 5 friends, come back with a revive item, cook 50 meals.

### 11.6 Social
- An Invite button in the HUD, and Friends tabs on every leaderboard with an "Invite Friends" button when they're empty.
- Leaderboards:
  - Fastest Descent.
  - Deepstones Earned.
  - Deepest Total.
- Stats panel:
  - Best Depth with a zone tag.
  - "Only X% got this far".

### 11.7 Other hooks
- **Group reward:** a free revive for joining the group. **Liking the game** should only be used after checking the current Roblox rules on incentivised votes.
- **Favorite prompt:** the first time a player returns to camp from a run, not on their first join.
- **The pet follows you** in camp and in the run.
