# Story: 100 Miles to Earth's Core

Nov 20, 2026. Our own story, told the way the reference tells its own (the frame analysis): a short skippable intro, then **Field Notes** (one line, a pencil sketch, signed by an expedition) and **Journal Pages** (a few lines from one named person) found at stops. Text lives in `src/shared/Story.luau`, which the game reads.

## The premise

On still nights, a pillar of gold light rises out of the great sinkhole by the mining town of **Sunfall**. The old folks call it the **Corelight**: the heart of the world, a hundred miles down, breathing slow.

Each breath soaks up through the rock, and the rock remembers it as **glowstone**.

For a hundred years, crews have drilled down to find the Corelight. Their lanterns went out one by one. Something down there **eats the light**: a tall, thin shadow the miners call **the Hollow**. Near the surface it only watches. Deeper down, it's hungrier.

Your crew takes the last drill rig down the hole, to reach the Core and light the **Core Beacon**, so the light rises all the way back up.

## The mystery (revealed a note at a time)

- **Early notes (zones 1–2):** the Hollow is a monster, the light is a treasure, and nobody comes back.
- **Middle (zones 3–4):**
  - The expeditions found the same things: an ancient people's carvings of a **lantern-bearer** who tended the Core.
  - The light "breathes", and the Hollow comes in the gaps between breaths.
- **Late (zones 5–6):**
  - The **Heart Keepers** tended the Core. One Keeper wanted the light for himself, put out his lantern to steal it, and the dark took him.
  - The Hollow is that Keeper. He hates every light he can't own.
  - Lighting the Core Beacon doesn't kill him. It gives him back his own light, and he's gone.

## Notes, by zone

Every zone has 6 Field Notes and 2 Journal Pages; the Core holds a 13th page. Notes are found on boards at stops ("[E] Read"), sometimes delved up, sometimes in wrecks.

### Zone 1: Sunfall Gorge (the Quarrymen)
**Field Notes**
1. "The light came up again last night. Gold, like a lamp left on at the bottom of the world." *(sketch: miners on the rim watching a pillar of light)*
2. "Rock here glows when you crack it. The kids call it glowstone. We call it pay." *(a pick splitting an amber stone)*
3. "Rig's ready. Two treads, one drill, four fools. We go down at sunrise." *(an old drill rig on the rim)*
4. "Lantern went out on its own today. No wind down here. No wind at all." *(a lantern with a thin line of smoke)*
5. "Saw something tall on the ledge above us. Thought it was a pillar. Pillars don't turn their heads." *(a tall shape on a ledge, two white dots)*
6. "Keep the lights burning. Whatever it is, it won't walk into the glow." *(a ring of lanterns around a fire)*

**Journal Pages**
1. *Mara Quill, quarry foreman:* "We dug for coal and found light. The deeper the seam, the brighter the stone, as if something below is pouring it up. Old Ned says it's breathing. Ned says a lot of things. But I've watched the glow in the walls rise and fall, slow, like a sleeper's chest."
2. *Mara Quill:* "Three crews went past the Drop. One came back, and they won't say what they saw. Only that it was tall, and it was patient, and it put out every lamp it could reach. I'm taking the next rig down. Someone has to find out where the light comes from."

### Zone 2: Glowshroom Grotto (the Lamp Keepers)
**Field Notes**
1. "Mushrooms the size of houses, glowing teal. You could read by them. We do." *(giant glowing mushrooms over a tiny rig)*
2. "Spores fall like snow. Don't breathe them in. Bram did. Bram is very cheerful now." *(a smiling miner under falling spores)*
3. "The glow dims every few hours, all at once, like someone pinching the lamps. We call it Lights Out." *(a cave going dark, lanterns still lit)*
4. "In the dark it comes closer. In the light it waits. Mind the light." *(a shadow just outside a lantern's circle)*
5. "Found Quarrymen tracks. They kept going down. So do we." *(boot prints by a dead lantern)*
6. "Feed the fire and the night goes faster. Don't ask me how. The light down here does what it likes." *(a crew around a bright fire, a clock spinning)*

**Journal Pages**
1. *Oren Vale, lamplighter:* "Every Lights Out, the mushrooms fade together, then brighten together. I timed it: the same rhythm as the glow in Sunfall's quarry walls. Whatever is breathing light up through the rock, its breath reaches this far. We're closer to its heart."
2. *Oren Vale:* "We lost two lamps tonight. Not broken: emptied. The glowstone inside was dull and grey, as if something drank it. I keep thinking of the old song: 'Hollow, Hollow, hungry and slow, drink up the lamplight, down you go.' I used to think it was for children."

### Zone 3: Crystal Hollows / Old Deepmine (the Ice Survey)
**Field Notes**
1. "Crystals ring when the drill passes, a note I can't name. Temperature: freezing. Morale: also freezing." *(a rig under huge crystal spires)*
2. "Light enters the crystal and doesn't come out for a long time. They hold the Core's breath." *(a crystal glowing from inside)*
3. "Carvings in the ice wall: a figure holding a lantern up to a great light. Not ours. Very old." *(a carved lantern-bearer)*
4. "The old Deepmine is here. Somebody dug this far, a long time before Sunfall had a name." *(rails and scaffolds in the dark)*
5. "The lift cage still works. Whoever left it wanted someone to follow." *(a lift cage over a black drop)*
6. "Measured the light's pulse: one breath every 3 hours and 11 minutes. The Hollow comes in the gap." *(a notebook with a wave drawn in it)*

**Journal Pages**
1. *Dr. Ilsa Brandt, Ice Survey:* "The carvings repeat: a lantern-bearer, a great light, a line of people bowing. Then one panel, scratched over, as if someone tried to erase it: the lantern-bearer turning away from the light, his lantern dark. I've copied it here. I don't know what it means. I don't like it."
2. *Dr. Ilsa Brandt:* "The Deepmine's builders left tools, food tins and a word painted on every beam: KEEP. Keep going? Keep the light? Keep out? My assistant says it's a name. 'Keepers.' He says they were here first. He says they're still here, in a way."

### Zone 4: Fossil Sea / Sporewood Shores (the Deep Fleet)
**Field Notes**
1. "A sea. Under the world. With a horizon. I'm a sailor and I still don't believe it." *(a tiny boat on a vast dark sea)*
2. "Something enormous died here once. We sail between its ribs." *(giant ribs arching over a causeway)*
3. "The geode overhead glows like a moon. Our compass spins. We steer by the light." *(a glowing geode above dark water)*
4. "Keep to the middle of the causeway. The edges remember the sea and want to go back to it." *(a causeway crumbling at the edges)*
5. "The jungle on the far shore breathes spores. Mask up, and sing loud so we can find each other." *(masked sailors in a mushroom jungle)*
6. "Saw the Hollow walk across the water toward us, slow. It stopped where our lantern light began." *(a shadow on the water at the edge of a light)*

**Journal Pages**
1. *Captain Teodor Reyes, the Deep Fleet:* "We found a shrine on an island: a stone lantern, cold, and a carving of a keeper kneeling to the Core. Under it, words in Sunfall's oldest script: 'He who carries the light must never keep it.' The crew joked it was a warning about pickpockets. Nobody's joking now."
2. *Captain Teodor Reyes:* "Half the fleet turned back today, and I don't blame them. The Hollow followed our wakes all night, close enough to see its face. It has no face. Just the shape of someone holding up a lantern that isn't there."

### Zone 5: Emberflow / Obsidian Rifts (the Ember Watch)
**Field Notes**
1. "The rock runs like honey down here, red and slow. Don't touch it. Don't even think about it." *(a lava river under a ledge)*
2. "Heat climbs fast away from cool stone. Eat the frost-fruit, drink the cold water, keep moving." *(a sweating miner biting a blue fruit)*
3. "Obsidian walls, glass-smooth. You see yourself in them. Sometimes you see someone behind you." *(a reflection with a tall shape behind it)*
4. "The Hollow hunts faster here. It's starving. The Core's breath is strong, and the gaps hurt it." *(a shadow sprinting along a rift)*
5. "We found a Keeper's lantern melted into the glass. Still warm. Hundreds of years, and still warm." *(an ornate lantern in black glass)*
6. "Don't run from it. Run to the light. It's the only place it can't follow." *(a crew sprinting toward a glowing Beacon)*

**Journal Pages**
1. *Sefa Okoro, the Ember Watch:* "The melted lantern had an inscription: 'The Keepers carry the Corelight up to the world, and give it all away.' Then, scratched in a rougher hand: 'Why should we give it away?' I think the one who wrote that is the one who turned his lantern dark. I think we know his name now. We've been calling it the Hollow."
2. *Sefa Okoro:* "He didn't steal the light. He tried, and the dark between the breaths took him instead: a Keeper with no light, forever hungry for it. Every lamp he drinks, he's trying to fill a lantern that's gone. I almost pity him. Almost. He took Jonah tonight."

### Zone 6: Heartstone Ruins / The Core Approach (the Heart Keepers)
**Field Notes**
1. "Temples of gold stone, built by people who carried light for a living. Their doors still open for a lantern." *(a temple door opening to a raised lantern)*
2. "Statues of Keepers line the road. Their lanterns are lit. Who lights them?" *(stone keepers holding glowing lanterns)*
3. "The light comes in beats now, close and loud. Move between them. Hold still in the dark and you'll be found." *(a crew pressed to a wall between pulses)*
4. "A Keeper's prayer, carved at every gate: 'Carry it up. Give it all. Keep nothing.'" *(an engraved gate)*
5. "The rock is turning to light. My hands glow when I touch the walls." *(a hand glowing against bright stone)*
6. "The Core Beacon stands cold at the bottom. It's waiting for someone to light it." *(a huge dark beacon under a blinding Core)*

**Journal Pages**
1. *Keeper Ashavel (translated):* "Each day we carry the Core's breath up through the stone, so the world above stays warm and lit. It is the only work. One of us has grown tired of giving. He asks why the light should go to people who will never thank us. I told him that is the whole point of light."
2. *Keeper Ashavel (translated):* "He has put out his lantern and gone into the dark between the breaths, to keep the light for himself. The dark has kept him instead. Now he hungers for every flame he sees. Light the Beacon, if you come after us. Give him back his light, and he will be at peace."

**Page 13, at the Core:** *Your own crew's page, written by the game with your crew's names.* "We lit the Beacon. The Core breathed out, and the light went up through a hundred miles of rock. Somewhere far above, in Sunfall, someone looked up and saw it. And the tall shadow on the ledge lifted its lantern, full of light at last, and was gone."
