100 Miles to Earth's Core: 1:1 UI mockup

Every screen copies the reference game's layout, sizes and colours, measured from the 2560×1440 recording (see docs/research/reference-ui/), and swaps in our game: a Drill Rig crew going down to the Earth's core, steered by cranking the tread on your side of the deck. Layout and gameplay structure follow the reference; resources, food, items, pets and names are our own (glowstone, delving, the Glow Mole, Deepstones). Icons are drawn in the reference's glossy style (docs/ui/icons/) and text uses its exact font, Comic Neue Angular Bold (Roblox "Cartoon"). The lobby screens follow the reference lobby recording (see docs/research/reference-lobby/). Sizes are in container units, so each 16:9 frame scales exactly. Backgrounds are placeholders; icons are flat stand-ins for the final 2D paintings.
